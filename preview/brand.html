Tally is a personal ledger. Warm graphite, soft chalk figures, and a single rose stroke for whatever needs your attention.

Logo

Four counted, one struck.

A tally is the oldest way to keep count of money owed. Four chalk strokes, then a fifth drawn across them in the accent colour. The diagonal also reads as a line trending up. In the wordmark, the bars are cut to the exact height of the two l’s.

Graphite, chalk, and one rose.

Surfaces step up in even lightness from the page to menus. Text is chalk, never pure white. The accent is reserved for the brand and the primary action, so when it appears it means something.
